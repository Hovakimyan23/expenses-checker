An intuitive, lightweight, and modern web application designed to help users track, analyze, and take full control of their daily expenses and personal finances.

Key Features
 Expense & Income Tracking — Easily log your daily transactions with categories, dates, and descriptions.

 Visual Analytics — Gain instant clarity on your spending habits through interactive visual charts and breakdowns.

 Budgeting & Goals — Set monthly spending limits and keep track of your savings target in real time.

 Fast & Responsive — Seamlessly optimized for both desktop and mobile screens.
